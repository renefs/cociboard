commit. Phase 2 of the approved mockups: the week grid, the phone week list, the planner
toolbar and the variety bar (ADR-0032). Gates:

## Review
- The DOM order is unchanged (day section, then its two slots), so keyboard navigation, the
  dnd ids, the regions and the accessible names stay; the matrix alignment comes from
  `grid-template-rows: subgrid` on the day sections and an aria-hidden row-label column.
- The protein and time line sits beside the slot button with the button's `::after`
  stretched over the card, so the whole card stays clickable and the accessible name still
  starts with the visible recipe name (WCAG 2.5.3).
- `PROTEIN_META` gets dark colours; the contrast test requires the dots to reach 3:1 on every
  surface in both themes. Poultry and dairy share a light colour (as before); the label next
  to the dot tells them apart on desktop.
- `WeekActionsMenu` reuses the SettingsMenu disclosure pattern. Copy last week and Clear week
  keep their names, disabled rules and the confirm dialog; E2E open the menu through a helper.
- "Suggest" became "Fill the week" / "Completar semana", which says what it does (it only fills
  empty slots). README, guide and specs follow.
- A real bug fixed on the way: `.slot--warning` and `.slot--highlight` were declared before the
  base `.slot` rule, which overrode their border and background.
- Accepted deviation: below about 1190px window width (the planner under 52rem) the desktop
  shows the compact list instead of seven columns of roughly 80px.
